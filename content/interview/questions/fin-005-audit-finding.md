# 审计发现整改 / Audit finding remediation

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你负责或参与整改审计发现的经历。你如何处理根因，而不是只补齐当期资料？

**English:** Tell me about remediating an audit finding. How did you address the root cause rather than only completing missing evidence for that period?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你如何判断整改设计和运行都有效？ / English: How did you test both design and operating effectiveness?
- 中文：如果业务认为控制成本过高，你如何回应？ / English: How did you respond if the business considered the control too costly?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 明确风险、控制目标、根因和受影响期间。 / Defines risk, control objective, root cause, and affected periods.
- 设计与风险相称且可持续的控制和证据。 / Designs proportionate, sustainable controls and evidence.
- 经独立复测关闭发现并监控残余风险。 / Closes through independent retesting and monitors residual risk.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：临时补签材料并视为完成。 / Backfills signatures and considers the issue closed.
- `3`：修订流程和培训，但有效性验证有限。 / Revises process and training with limited effectiveness testing.
- `5`：根因整改、明确所有权、复测运行效果并平衡控制成本。 / Fixes root cause, assigns ownership, retests operation, and balances control cost.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 隐瞒样本例外以按时关闭。 / Hides sample exceptions to close on time.
- 过度控制导致业务绕行。 / Over-controls until the business routes around the process.
