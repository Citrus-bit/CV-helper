# 品牌与效果预算 / Brand and performance budget

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 短期获客成本上升，管理层希望把品牌预算全部转为效果广告。你会如何评估并提出方案？

**English:** Acquisition cost rises, and leadership wants to move all brand budget into performance ads. How would you evaluate and respond?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：如何识别效果广告中的品牌溢出和自然需求？ / English: How would you identify brand spillover and organic demand in performance results?
- 中文：预算必须削减时怎样保护学习价值？ / English: If budget must be cut, how would you preserve learning value?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 区分短期可归因转化和长期增量需求。 / Separates attributable short-term conversion from long-term incremental demand.
- 使用地区/时间实验、搜索趋势和品牌健康指标。 / Uses geo/time tests, search trends, and brand-health metrics.
- 提出组合预算、决策窗口和再分配规则。 / Proposes a portfolio, decision window, and reallocation rules.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：以“品牌无法衡量”或单一 ROAS 决策。 / Decides that brand is immeasurable or uses only ROAS.
- `3`：同时考虑长短期指标，但增量验证有限。 / Considers long- and short-term metrics with limited incrementality testing.
- `5`：识别渠道互相影响、设计增量实验，并按边际回报动态分配。 / Accounts for channel interaction, tests incrementality, and allocates by marginal return.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 把相关性报表当作因果证据。 / Treats attribution reports as causal proof.
- 频繁改预算导致实验无法解释。 / Changes budgets too frequently to interpret results.
