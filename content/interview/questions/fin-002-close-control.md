# 月结提速但不降质 / Faster close without weaker controls

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你缩短月结周期，同时保持或提高控制质量的经历。

**English:** Tell me about a time you shortened the month-end close while maintaining or improving control quality.

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：哪些步骤可以提前，哪些必须等到期末？ / English: Which tasks could move earlier, and which had to remain at period-end?
- 中文：你用什么证据确认提速没有增加错报？ / English: What evidence showed speed did not increase misstatement risk?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 分析关键路径、等待、返工和重大性。 / Analyzes critical path, waiting, rework, and materiality.
- 使用标准清单、自动对账、复核证据和例外升级。 / Uses standard checklists, automated reconciliations, review evidence, and exception escalation.
- 同时报告天数、调整数、迟报和控制例外。 / Reports days, adjustments, late items, and control exceptions together.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：通过跳过复核或延后问题实现提速。 / Speeds up by skipping review or deferring issues.
- `3`：重排流程并缩短周期，但质量验证较少。 / Reorders work and shortens close with limited quality validation.
- `5`：重构关键路径、自动化可控步骤、强化例外控制并持续验证质量。 / Redesigns the path, automates controlled work, strengthens exceptions, and verifies quality.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 将未解决差异滚入下月。 / Rolls unresolved differences into the next month.
- 自动化缺少访问和变更控制。 / Automates without access or change controls.
