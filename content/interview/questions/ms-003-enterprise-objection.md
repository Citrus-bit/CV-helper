# 企业客户异议 / Enterprise objection handling

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 客户说：“你们价格比现有方案高 30%，而且迁移有风险。”请说明你会怎样回应，而不是立即打折。

**English:** A customer says, “You cost 30% more than our current solution, and migration is risky.” Explain how you would respond without immediately discounting.

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你需要先确认哪些成本和决策标准？ / English: Which costs and decision criteria would you clarify first?
- 中文：什么情况下你会承认产品不适合？ / English: When would you acknowledge that the product is not a fit?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 先探索现状成本、风险来源、成功标准和决策流程。 / Explores current cost, risk sources, success criteria, and decision process.
- 用可验证价值、试点和迁移保障回应。 / Responds with verifiable value, a pilot, and migration safeguards.
- 愿意在不匹配时退出，不作无法兑现承诺。 / Willing to disqualify rather than overpromise.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：立刻降价或贬低竞品。 / Discounts immediately or disparages competitors.
- `3`：能提问并阐述价值，但量化和风险计划有限。 / Discovers and explains value with limited quantification or risk planning.
- `5`：重构总成本、共同验证价值、降低迁移风险并确认双向适配。 / Reframes total cost, validates value jointly, reduces migration risk, and confirms mutual fit.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 承诺尚不存在的功能或安全认证。 / Promises nonexistent features or certifications.
- 未经授权引用其他客户敏感数据。 / Uses another customer's confidential data without permission.
