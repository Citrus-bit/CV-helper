# 业务伙伴中的反对意见 / Constructive challenge in finance

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你不同意业务团队的增长或投资假设，但仍需要保持合作的经历。

**English:** Tell me about a time you challenged a business team's growth or investment assumptions while preserving the partnership.

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你如何区分保守偏好与真正的财务风险？ / English: How did you separate conservative preference from real financial risk?
- 中文：最终决定与结果是什么？ / English: What was the final decision and outcome?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 先理解业务目标和假设来源。 / Understands business goals and assumption sources first.
- 用情景、敏感性和验证里程碑提出建设性质疑。 / Challenges constructively with scenarios, sensitivities, and validation milestones.
- 即使决定不同，也建立监控和复查机制。 / Establishes monitoring and review even if another decision is chosen.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：只以“财务不批准”否决。 / Rejects with “finance will not approve.”
- `3`：提出数据和替代方案并达成决定。 / Brings data and alternatives and reaches a decision.
- `5`：共同重构假设、量化下行、设计分阶段投入并维护长期信任。 / Reframes assumptions jointly, quantifies downside, stages investment, and preserves trust.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 为维持关系忽略重大风险。 / Ignores material risk to preserve the relationship.
- 使用财务术语压制业务讨论。 / Uses finance jargon to shut down discussion.
