# 可验证的知识交接 / Verifiable knowledge handover

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你必须把关键工作交接给他人的经历。你如何确认对方能独立处理，而不只是“看过文档”？

**English:** Tell me about a critical handover. How did you confirm the recipient could operate independently rather than merely having read the documentation?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你如何识别最容易遗漏的隐性知识？ / English: How did you identify tacit knowledge most likely to be missed?
- 中文：交接后出现了什么问题，你怎样改进材料？ / English: What issue surfaced afterward, and how did you improve the material?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 根据风险区分清单、演练、影随和反向讲解。 / Uses checklists, drills, shadowing, and teach-back according to risk.
- 明确所有权、升级路径和完成标准。 / Defines ownership, escalation paths, and completion criteria.
- 用独立执行或故障演练验证交接。 / Validates handover through independent execution or a failure drill.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只发送文件，没有确认理解或责任。 / Sends files without confirming understanding or ownership.
- `3`：有文档、讲解和基本确认，但缺少实际演练。 / Provides documentation and walkthroughs with limited practical validation.
- `5`：按风险设计交接、实操验证、明确升级，并根据反馈更新知识。 / Designs by risk, validates in practice, clarifies escalation, and improves the knowledge base.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 交接依赖个人长期在线兜底。 / Relies on the original owner remaining indefinitely available.
- 文档包含不必要的密钥或敏感数据。 / Includes unnecessary credentials or sensitive data.
