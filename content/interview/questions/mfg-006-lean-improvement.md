# 一线参与的精益改善 / Frontline-led lean improvement

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你与一线员工共同改善作业流程的经历。你如何确保改善持续，而不是活动结束后反弹？

**English:** Tell me about improving a process with frontline employees. How did you make the gain last beyond the improvement event?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：一线经验如何改变了你的初始方案？ / English: How did frontline knowledge change your initial idea?
- 中文：你用哪些领先指标发现反弹？ / English: Which leading indicators revealed regression?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 现场观察真实工作并尊重操作人员知识。 / Observes actual work and respects operator knowledge.
- 小步试验，同时测量安全、质量、交付和负担。 / Tests small changes while measuring safety, quality, delivery, and burden.
- 更新标准作业、培训、可视管理和审核节奏。 / Updates standard work, training, visual management, and audit cadence.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：外部设计方案后要求一线照做。 / Designs externally and tells operators to comply.
- `3`：共同改善并取得指标结果，但维持机制有限。 / Co-improves with measurable results but limited sustainment.
- `5`：共同定义问题、验证改进、嵌入日常管理并证明长期稳定。 / Co-defines, validates, embeds daily management, and proves sustained performance.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 以减少人员为唯一改善目标。 / Treats headcount reduction as the only goal.
- 忽略标准作业中的合理例外。 / Ignores legitimate exceptions to standard work.
