# 关键相关方分歧 / Stakeholder disagreement

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你与关键相关方在方案上有实质分歧的经历。你如何找到可检验的共同基础并作出决定？

**English:** Tell me about a substantive disagreement with a key stakeholder. How did you establish testable common ground and reach a decision?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：如果最终没有采用你的方案，你做了什么？ / English: What did you do if your proposal was not selected?
- 中文：什么证据最可能改变你的观点？ / English: What evidence would have changed your view?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 准确复述对方目标，而非把分歧人格化。 / Represents the other party's goals accurately without personalizing conflict.
- 用数据、试点或决策原则缩小不确定性。 / Reduces uncertainty with data, pilots, or decision principles.
- 决策后支持共同结果并记录学习。 / Commits after the decision and records what was learned.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：依赖职位压服、回避冲突或贬低对方。 / Relies on authority, avoids conflict, or disparages the other party.
- `3`：进行了理性讨论并达成决定，但证据或复盘有限。 / Reaches a reasoned decision with limited evidence or reflection.
- `5`：建立共同目标、设计验证方式、明确决策权并维护后续合作。 / Aligns goals, tests assumptions, clarifies decision rights, and preserves the relationship.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 把妥协本身当成成功，不说明结果。 / Treats compromise itself as success without an outcome.
- 暴露前同事身份或敏感争议。 / Exposes identities or confidential disputes.
