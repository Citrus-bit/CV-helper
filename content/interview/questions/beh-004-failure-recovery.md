# 失败与恢复 / Failure and recovery

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你的判断或执行导致结果未达预期的经历。你如何止损、承担责任并防止重演？

**English:** Describe a time your judgment or execution led to a poor outcome. How did you contain the impact, take responsibility, and prevent recurrence?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你最早错过了什么信号？ / English: What early signal did you miss?
- 中文：后来改变的是个人习惯还是系统机制？ / English: Did you change a personal habit, a system, or both?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 清楚区分自己的责任与外部条件。 / Clearly separates personal responsibility from external conditions.
- 先保护客户或业务，再进行根因分析。 / Protects customers or operations before analyzing root cause.
- 给出可验证的防复发措施和后续结果。 / Provides verifiable prevention measures and later outcomes.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：回避责任，或没有采取修复行动。 / Avoids responsibility or takes no corrective action.
- `3`：承担责任并修复，但根因或防复发机制较弱。 / Owns and fixes the issue but has weak root-cause or prevention work.
- `5`：快速止损、透明沟通、查明根因并验证系统性改进。 / Contains impact, communicates openly, finds root cause, and validates systemic improvement.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 讲述仍受保密约束的敏感细节。 / Reveals confidential details.
- 把“从未失败”当作优势。 / Presents never having failed as a strength.
