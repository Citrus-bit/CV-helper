# 产能瓶颈 / Capacity bottleneck

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 订单增长后产线加班增加，但产出提升有限、在制品持续上升。你会如何找出并改善真实瓶颈？

**English:** Orders rise, overtime increases, output barely improves, and work-in-process grows. How would you find and improve the real bottleneck?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你会观察周期时间、利用率和排队的哪些关系？ / English: What relationships among cycle time, utilization, and queues would you examine?
- 中文：改善后瓶颈转移时如何处理？ / English: What would you do when the bottleneck moves?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 用流量、节拍、等待、换型、停机和良率验证约束点。 / Uses flow, takt, waiting, changeover, downtime, and yield to verify constraint.
- 优先保护和利用约束，再同步上下游节奏。 / Protects and exploits the constraint before synchronizing upstream and downstream.
- 关注总产出、交付和在制品，而非局部设备利用率。 / Optimizes throughput, delivery, and WIP rather than local utilization.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：所有工序同时加人或加设备。 / Adds labor or equipment everywhere.
- `3`：用数据找到瓶颈并改善，但系统节奏和持续性有限。 / Finds and improves a bottleneck with limited system synchronization or sustainment.
- `5`：验证约束、低成本释放能力、控制 WIP，并持续识别转移后的新瓶颈。 / Validates the constraint, unlocks capacity economically, controls WIP, and manages the moving bottleneck.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 以高利用率制造更多无需求在制品。 / Creates unneeded WIP to maximize utilization.
- 忽略维护、安全或质量换取产量。 / Sacrifices maintenance, safety, or quality for output.
