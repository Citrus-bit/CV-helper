# 预测偏差分析 / Forecast variance analysis

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 月度利润低于预测，但收入高于预测。请说明你会如何拆解偏差并向业务负责人解释。

**English:** Monthly profit is below forecast while revenue is above forecast. Explain how you would decompose the variance and communicate it to business leaders.

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你如何区分价格、销量、结构、时点和一次性因素？ / English: How would you separate price, volume, mix, timing, and one-off effects?
- 中文：哪些偏差需要更新全年预测？ / English: Which variances should change the full-year forecast?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 先核对口径、期间、数据完整性和可比基线。 / Validates definitions, period, completeness, and baseline.
- 建立收入到利润的可解释桥接。 / Builds an explainable bridge from revenue to profit.
- 区分已发生解释、未来风险和可执行动作。 / Separates historical explanation, forward risk, and actions.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只说成本上升，没有拆解或验证。 / Says costs rose without decomposition or validation.
- `3`：完成主要偏差桥接，但驱动因素或预测影响有限。 / Bridges major variances with limited driver or forecast implications.
- `5`：验证数据、量化驱动与持续性，并把分析转成责任明确的行动。 / Validates data, quantifies drivers and persistence, and converts insight into owned actions.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 用相互抵消的总额掩盖重大单项风险。 / Lets offsetting totals hide material line-item risks.
- 未说明估计和实际值的差异。 / Fails to distinguish estimates from actuals.
