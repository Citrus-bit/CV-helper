# 流动性压力管理 / Managing liquidity pressure

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 销售增长但回款变慢，未来八周可能出现现金缺口。你会如何建立视图、排序行动并管理风险？

**English:** Sales are growing but collections are slowing, creating a possible cash gap in eight weeks. How would you build the view, prioritize actions, and manage risk?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：如何验证现金预测而不依赖单一最佳估计？ / English: How would you validate the cash forecast beyond one best estimate?
- 中文：哪些行动会伤害长期业务，如何权衡？ / English: Which actions could damage the long-term business, and how would you weigh them?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 建立滚动现金预测并核对应收、应付和承诺。 / Builds a rolling cash forecast and reconciles receivables, payables, and commitments.
- 按可控性、速度、影响和关系风险排序措施。 / Ranks actions by control, speed, impact, and relationship risk.
- 设置触发点、责任人和多情景融资/运营选项。 / Sets triggers, owners, and financing or operating options by scenario.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只建议延迟所有付款。 / Suggests delaying every payment.
- `3`：建立预测并推进回款、成本措施，但情景和触发点有限。 / Forecasts and acts on collections and cost with limited scenarios or triggers.
- `5`：核实现金来源、分层行动、保护关键关系并提前准备应急资金。 / Verifies cash sources, sequences actions, protects critical relationships, and prepares contingency funding.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 违反合同随意延付或挪用受限资金。 / Delays contractually or misuses restricted cash.
- 将会计利润等同于可用现金。 / Equates accounting profit with available cash.
