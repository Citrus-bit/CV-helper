# 市场与销售的线索质量 / Aligning on lead quality

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 市场称线索量创新高，销售却认为线索无效。你如何建立共同定义并找出真正问题？

**English:** Marketing reports record lead volume while sales says the leads are poor. How would you establish shared definitions and find the real issue?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你会追踪哪些从来源到收入的连接指标？ / English: Which linked metrics would you track from source to revenue?
- 中文：怎样避免定义被某一团队操纵？ / English: How would you keep definitions from being gamed by one team?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 建立可观察的资格标准、响应 SLA 和拒绝原因。 / Establishes observable qualification, response SLA, and rejection reasons.
- 按来源、客群、处理速度和后续转化分析。 / Analyzes source, segment, response time, and downstream conversion.
- 通过共同实验和收入结果迭代定义。 / Evolves definitions through joint tests and revenue outcomes.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：站队并要求另一方“提高质量/跟进”。 / Takes sides and tells the other team to improve.
- `3`：统一 MQL/SQL 定义并建看板，但激励问题有限。 / Aligns definitions and dashboards with limited incentive analysis.
- `5`：打通端到端数据、修正激励、验证处理行为并持续校准。 / Connects end-to-end data, fixes incentives, validates behavior, and calibrates continuously.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 只提高门槛导致有效但早期的线索被丢弃。 / Raises thresholds and discards valid early-stage leads.
- 在没有用户同意时扩展数据用途。 / Expands data use without user consent.
