# 客户流失预警 / Customer churn signal

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 一个重要客户的使用量逐月下降，但联系人仍表示“没有问题”。你会如何判断风险并采取行动？

**English:** A key customer's usage declines month over month, while the contact says everything is fine. How would you assess risk and act?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你会区分季节性、价值下降和组织变化吗？ / English: How would you separate seasonality, value decline, and organizational change?
- 中文：哪些干预可能反而加速流失？ / English: Which interventions could make churn more likely?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 结合使用深度、关键工作流、支持记录、组织和合同节点。 / Combines usage depth, key workflows, support, organization, and contract timing.
- 用开放式业务复盘验证假设，而非制造恐慌。 / Tests hypotheses through an open business review without alarmism.
- 共同定义恢复价值的行动、负责人和检查点。 / Co-defines value recovery actions, owners, and checkpoints.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：立即推销升级或频繁催促客户。 / Immediately upsells or repeatedly pressures the customer.
- `3`：检查数据并安排沟通，但价值恢复计划有限。 / Reviews data and engages the customer with a limited recovery plan.
- `5`：多信号诊断、验证客户目标、重建价值路径并诚实更新预测。 / Diagnoses multiple signals, validates goals, restores value, and updates forecasts honestly.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 把个人用户行为用于未经授权的监控。 / Uses individual behavior for unauthorized surveillance.
- 隐瞒风险以保护续约预测。 / Hides risk to protect a renewal forecast.
