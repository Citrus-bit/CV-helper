# 压力下的职业判断 / Professional judgment under pressure

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次进度或业绩压力与合规、客户利益或数据真实性发生冲突的经历。你如何处理？

**English:** Describe a time delivery or commercial pressure conflicted with compliance, customer interests, or data integrity. How did you respond?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你依据了哪些政策或原则？ / English: Which policies or principles guided you?
- 中文：你如何在不扩大损害的情况下升级问题？ / English: How did you escalate without increasing harm?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 识别受影响群体、不可逆风险和授权边界。 / Identifies affected parties, irreversible risks, and authority boundaries.
- 保留事实记录并使用适当升级渠道。 / Preserves factual records and uses appropriate escalation channels.
- 提出可行替代方案，而非只说“不”。 / Offers a viable alternative instead of only refusing.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：为达成目标隐瞒、篡改或绕过控制。 / Conceals, alters, or bypasses controls to meet a target.
- `3`：坚持底线并升级，但替代方案或沟通有限。 / Holds the line and escalates, with limited alternatives or communication.
- `5`：基于政策保护相关方，证据化升级并促成合规替代方案。 / Protects stakeholders, escalates with evidence, and enables a compliant alternative.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 披露可识别的举报或调查信息。 / Reveals identifiable whistleblowing or investigation details.
- 把个人偏好误称为法规要求。 / Presents a personal preference as a legal requirement.
