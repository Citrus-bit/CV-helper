# 无直接职权的影响 / Influencing without authority

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你需要推动不向你汇报的团队改变做法的经历。你如何理解他们的动机并形成承诺？

**English:** Tell me about a time you needed a team outside your reporting line to change its approach. How did you understand its incentives and build commitment?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：对方最合理的顾虑是什么？ / English: What was the other team's most reasonable concern?
- 中文：你如何区分真正认同与表面同意？ / English: How did you distinguish real commitment from polite agreement?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 先理解对方目标、成本和约束。 / Starts with the other team's goals, costs, and constraints.
- 用共同指标、试点或互惠资源推动合作。 / Uses shared metrics, a pilot, or reciprocal support.
- 描述持续采用的证据，而非一次会议结果。 / Shows sustained adoption, not just a meeting outcome.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：依赖高层施压或把对方描述为阻碍。 / Relies on executive pressure or frames others as obstacles.
- `3`：通过沟通获得配合，但动机和持续性证据有限。 / Gains cooperation with limited insight into incentives or durability.
- `5`：对齐利益、降低采用成本、验证承诺并建立长期合作。 / Aligns incentives, lowers adoption cost, validates commitment, and builds durable collaboration.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 将操纵或隐瞒信息描述为影响力。 / Describes manipulation or withheld information as influence.
- 抢占跨团队成果。 / Takes sole credit for cross-team results.
