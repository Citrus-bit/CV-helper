# 冲突优先级 / Conflicting priorities

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你同时收到多个紧急任务的经历。你如何判断先后顺序，并让相关方接受这个安排？

**English:** Tell me about a time you received several urgent requests at once. How did you prioritize them and gain stakeholder acceptance?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你使用了哪些信息判断影响与时限？ / English: What information did you use to judge impact and deadlines?
- 中文：如果关键相关方不同意，你会怎样调整？ / English: How would you adapt if a key stakeholder disagreed?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 明确区分紧急度、业务影响和依赖关系。 / Separates urgency, business impact, and dependencies.
- 主动说明取舍、风险和重新承诺的时间。 / Communicates trade-offs, risks, and revised commitments.
- 用可验证结果说明排序有效。 / Uses a verifiable outcome to show the prioritization worked.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只说“都加班做完”，没有判断依据或沟通。 / Says they simply worked longer, with no rationale or communication.
- `3`：说明了排序方法和基本结果，但缺少量化影响或复盘。 / Explains a method and result but lacks measured impact or reflection.
- `5`：以证据排序、协商范围、管理风险，并总结可复用机制。 / Uses evidence, negotiates scope, manages risk, and creates a reusable mechanism.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 把长期加班当作唯一解决方案。 / Treats chronic overtime as the only solution.
- 未经沟通擅自延后他人工作。 / Delays others' work without communicating.
