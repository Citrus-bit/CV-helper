# 不确定条件下的投资评估 / Investment under uncertainty

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 公司考虑建设一项三年回收的能力，但需求、建设成本和残值都不确定。你会如何分析并提出决策建议？

**English:** A company is considering an investment with a three-year payback, but demand, build cost, and residual value are uncertain. How would you analyze and recommend?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：哪些假设最值得优先验证？ / English: Which assumptions deserve validation first?
- 中文：如何给分阶段投资或退出选择定价？ / English: How would you value staged investment or exit options?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 建立现金流、资本成本、税务、营运资本和情景。 / Models cash flow, cost of capital, tax, working capital, and scenarios.
- 用敏感性和盈亏平衡识别关键假设。 / Uses sensitivity and break-even analysis to identify critical assumptions.
- 考虑战略选择权、能力约束和非财务风险。 / Considers strategic options, capacity constraints, and nonfinancial risk.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：只看简单回收期或单一乐观情景。 / Uses only simple payback or one optimistic scenario.
- `3`：完成 NPV 与敏感性分析，但决策选项有限。 / Builds NPV and sensitivity analysis with limited decision options.
- `5`：透明建模不确定性、验证致命假设、设计分阶段承诺并说明决策边界。 / Models uncertainty transparently, tests fatal assumptions, stages commitment, and states decision limits.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 用高精度数字掩盖脆弱假设。 / Uses precise numbers to mask fragile assumptions.
- 忽略退出成本、容量爬坡或合规成本。 / Ignores exit, ramp-up, or compliance cost.
