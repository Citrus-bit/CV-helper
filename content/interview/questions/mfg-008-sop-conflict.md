# 产销计划冲突 / Sales and operations planning conflict

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 销售预测大幅上调，工厂认为不可信，采购需要立即锁定长交期物料。你如何推动一次可执行的 S&OP 决策？

**English:** Sales sharply raises the forecast, the plant doubts it, and procurement must commit long-lead material now. How would you drive an executable S&OP decision?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你如何量化上行机会和下行库存风险？ / English: How would you quantify upside opportunity and downside inventory risk?
- 中文：最终谁对共识计划和例外负责？ / English: Who owns the consensus plan and exceptions?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 对齐一套需求、供应、库存和财务事实。 / Aligns one fact base for demand, supply, inventory, and finance.
- 使用情景、概率、分阶段采购和客户优先级。 / Uses scenarios, probabilities, staged buys, and customer priority.
- 明确决策权、冻结窗口、触发点和例外成本。 / Defines decision rights, frozen windows, triggers, and exception costs.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：简单平均销售和工厂数字。 / Averages the sales and plant numbers.
- `3`：形成共识预测并识别风险，但触发机制有限。 / Reaches consensus and identifies risk with limited triggers.
- `5`：量化不确定性、设计可逆承诺、明确治理并跟踪预测偏差。 / Quantifies uncertainty, stages reversible commitments, defines governance, and tracks bias.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 用“共识”隐藏无人承担的决定。 / Uses consensus to hide unowned decisions.
- 忽略供应商最小批量和取消责任。 / Ignores minimum buys and cancellation liability.
