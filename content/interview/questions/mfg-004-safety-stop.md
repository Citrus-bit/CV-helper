# 安全停线判断 / Safety stop-work decision

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 交付压力很大时，你发现设备防护装置偶发失效，但尚未造成人员伤害。你会如何处理？

**English:** Under intense delivery pressure, you discover an intermittent machine-guard failure that has not yet caused injury. What do you do?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：恢复生产前需要哪些验证和批准？ / English: What validation and approval are needed before restart?
- 中文：如何与客户和管理层沟通停线影响？ / English: How would you communicate the shutdown impact to customers and leadership?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 立即停止暴露、隔离能源并按安全程序升级。 / Stops exposure, isolates energy, and escalates through safety procedures.
- 保护现场证据，评估类似设备和潜在暴露范围。 / Preserves evidence and checks similar equipment and exposure.
- 只有工程修复、风险评估和授权完成后复产。 / Restarts only after engineering correction, risk assessment, and authorization.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：因未发生事故而继续生产。 / Continues because no injury has occurred.
- `3`：停机并修复设备，但范围检查或复产验证有限。 / Stops and repairs with limited scope review or restart validation.
- `5`：执行停工权、系统评估暴露、验证控制并透明管理交付影响。 / Exercises stop-work authority, assesses exposure systemically, validates controls, and manages delivery transparently.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 临时旁路安全联锁。 / Bypasses a safety interlock temporarily.
- 将发现问题的员工视为造成延误。 / Blames the person who raised the concern.
