# 将反馈转化为改变 / Turning feedback into change

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次你收到难以接受但最终有价值的反馈。你采取了什么具体行动，后来有什么变化？

**English:** Describe a piece of difficult but valuable feedback you received. What did you change, and what happened afterward?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你如何判断这条反馈值得采纳？ / English: How did you decide the feedback was worth acting on?
- 中文：你用什么证据确认自己真的改善了？ / English: What evidence showed that you had improved?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 不回避最初反应，但能区分情绪与事实。 / Acknowledges the initial reaction while separating emotion from facts.
- 描述具体行为实验而非泛泛“更加努力”。 / Describes a concrete behavior change rather than simply trying harder.
- 有后续反馈、指标或工作结果作为验证。 / Provides follow-up feedback, metrics, or work outcomes.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：否认需要改进，或把问题完全归咎于他人。 / Denies any need to improve or blames others entirely.
- `3`：接受反馈并改变做法，但验证和复盘有限。 / Acts on feedback but offers limited validation or reflection.
- `5`：验证反馈、设计改变、跟踪效果，并把经验应用到新场景。 / Validates feedback, tests a change, tracks results, and transfers the lesson.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 选择无关痛痒的“伪缺点”。 / Chooses a harmless disguised strength.
- 声称改变但没有行为或结果证据。 / Claims change without behavioral or outcome evidence.
