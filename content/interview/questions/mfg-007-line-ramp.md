# 新产线爬坡 / New-line ramp-up

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 新产线试产良率达标，但节拍和换型稳定性不足。你会如何制定爬坡计划并决定何时增加产量？

**English:** A new line meets pilot yield targets but lacks stable cycle time and changeovers. How would you plan ramp-up and decide when to increase volume?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：哪些参数需要能力指数或趋势控制？ / English: Which parameters need capability or trend controls?
- 中文：如何避免试产专家长期成为隐性依赖？ / English: How would you avoid permanent dependency on pilot experts?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 设定安全、良率、节拍、停机、人员能力和质量门槛。 / Sets gates for safety, yield, cycle, downtime, skills, and quality.
- 以分阶段负载、挑战批次和换型演练验证稳定性。 / Uses staged load, challenge lots, and changeover drills.
- 管理问题清单、所有者、反应计划和停止规则。 / Manages issues, owners, reaction plans, and stop rules.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：以单次良率达标直接满产。 / Moves to full volume from one good yield result.
- `3`：有阶段计划和主要指标，但人员与异常验证有限。 / Has stages and metrics with limited people or exception validation.
- `5`：多维门槛、统计稳定性、标准化能力和可执行的降速/停线规则。 / Uses multidimensional gates, statistical stability, standardized capability, and actionable slowdown/stop rules.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 为达产关闭异常报警或放宽规格。 / Disables alarms or loosens specification to hit volume.
- 忽略维护备件和校准能力。 / Ignores maintenance spares and calibration capability.
