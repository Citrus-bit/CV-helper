# 异常付款与舞弊风险 / Suspicious payment and fraud risk

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 你发现一个供应商银行账户在紧急付款前刚被修改，申请人要求跳过回拨验证。你会如何处理？

**English:** A vendor bank account was changed just before an urgent payment, and the requester asks to skip callback verification. What would you do?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：如何保全证据又避免不当指控？ / English: How would you preserve evidence without making an improper accusation?
- 中文：如果请求来自高层，处理是否不同？ / English: Would your response change if the request came from an executive?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 暂停不可逆付款，使用独立可信渠道验证。 / Pauses the irreversible payment and verifies through an independent trusted channel.
- 遵循职责分离、升级和事件记录。 / Follows segregation, escalation, and incident documentation.
- 事后检查相似变更并改进控制。 / Reviews similar changes and improves controls afterward.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：因紧急或高层要求而绕过控制。 / Bypasses controls due to urgency or seniority.
- `3`：停止付款并核验，但证据和后续范围有限。 / Stops and verifies with limited evidence preservation or wider review.
- `5`：安全暂停、独立验证、保密升级、保护证据并开展范围化整改。 / Pauses safely, verifies independently, escalates discreetly, preserves evidence, and scopes remediation.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 直接联系可疑邮件中提供的新号码。 / Calls the new number supplied in the suspicious message.
- 未经调查公开指控个人舞弊。 / Publicly accuses an individual before investigation.
