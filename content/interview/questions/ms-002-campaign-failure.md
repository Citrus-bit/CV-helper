# 营销活动未达标 / Campaign underperformance

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次营销活动明显未达目标的经历。你如何判断问题来自策略、创意、渠道、受众还是测量？

**English:** Tell me about a campaign that materially missed its goal. How did you distinguish strategy, creative, channel, audience, and measurement issues?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：活动进行中你看到了哪些早期信号？ / English: Which early signals were visible during the campaign?
- 中文：下一轮你只保留或改变了什么？ / English: What did you keep or change in the next iteration?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 使用目标、基线、漏斗和对照进行诊断。 / Uses goals, baselines, funnel stages, and controls.
- 承担可控决策，不把失败只归因于市场。 / Owns controllable decisions instead of blaming the market.
- 用下一轮结果验证学习。 / Validates learning in the next iteration.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：只说预算不够或用户不理解。 / Blames budget or users without analysis.
- `3`：能定位主要问题并调整，但归因证据有限。 / Finds a likely issue and adapts with limited attribution evidence.
- `5`：按漏斗拆解假设、及时止损、控制下一轮变量并验证改善。 / Decomposes hypotheses, contains spend, controls the next test, and verifies improvement.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 选择性报告好看的互动指标。 / Cherry-picks favorable engagement metrics.
- 为补量触达不合适或未授权人群。 / Targets inappropriate or non-consenting audiences to fill volume.
