# 库存与服务水平平衡 / Balancing inventory and service

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 库存金额过高但关键 SKU 仍频繁缺货。你如何诊断并重新设置补货策略？

**English:** Inventory value is high while critical SKUs still stock out frequently. How would you diagnose and redesign replenishment?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你会如何处理间歇需求和长交期？ / English: How would you handle intermittent demand and long lead times?
- 中文：怎样区分预测问题与参数或执行问题？ / English: How would you separate forecast, parameter, and execution issues?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 按价值、关键性、需求波动、交期和可替代性分层。 / Segments by value, criticality, variability, lead time, and substitutability.
- 核对预测偏差、安全库存、批量、主数据和供应可靠性。 / Reviews forecast error, safety stock, lot sizes, master data, and supply reliability.
- 用服务、现金、呆滞和加急成本共同优化。 / Optimizes service, cash, obsolescence, and expedite cost together.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：统一降低库存目标。 / Reduces every inventory target uniformly.
- `3`：完成 ABC 分类并调整参数，但动态验证有限。 / Uses ABC and adjusts parameters with limited dynamic validation.
- `5`：多维分层、修复数据与策略根因、模拟服务影响并持续重算。 / Segments multidimensionally, fixes data and policy causes, simulates service impact, and recalculates continuously.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 忽略关键备件低频但高损失的特性。 / Ignores low-frequency, high-consequence critical spares.
- 用大量加急运输掩盖计划问题。 / Masks planning problems with repeated expediting.
