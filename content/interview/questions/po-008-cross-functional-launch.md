# 跨职能上线 / Cross-functional launch

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 请讲一次涉及产品、技术、法务、客服或销售等多个团队的上线。你如何管理依赖和准备度？

**English:** Tell me about a launch involving several functions such as product, engineering, legal, support, or sales. How did you manage dependencies and readiness?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你如何定义“准备完成”，而不是依赖主观绿灯？ / English: How did you define readiness beyond subjective green lights?
- 中文：哪个依赖最晚暴露，你学到了什么？ / English: Which dependency surfaced latest, and what did you learn?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 建立关键路径、依赖所有者、证据化门槛和升级节奏。 / Establishes critical path, owners, evidence-based gates, and escalation cadence.
- 同时准备客户沟通、支持材料和回滚。 / Prepares customer communication, support, and rollback.
- 说明自己贡献和团队成果的边界。 / Distinguishes personal contribution from team results.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：只列会议或工具，没有决策与结果。 / Lists meetings or tools without decisions or outcomes.
- `3`：有计划、负责人和上线结果，但准备度证据有限。 / Has a plan, owners, and result with limited readiness evidence.
- `5`：以关键路径和硬门槛管理依赖，提前演练故障并复盘改进。 / Manages dependencies with gates, rehearses failure, and improves after launch.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 用频繁会议替代明确责任。 / Substitutes frequent meetings for clear ownership.
- 隐瞒未完成项以维持发布日期。 / Conceals incomplete work to preserve a date.
