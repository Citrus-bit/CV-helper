# 工程变更控制 / Engineering change control

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次设计、材料或工艺变更必须快速导入生产的经历。你如何控制版本、验证和库存切换？

**English:** Tell me about rapidly introducing a design, material, or process change into production. How did you control versions, validation, and inventory transition?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：哪些功能、质量或合规方必须批准？ / English: Which functional, quality, or regulatory approvals were required?
- 中文：如何防止新旧版本混用？ / English: How did you prevent mixed-version production?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 明确变更原因、影响对象、风险、有效日期和序列范围。 / Defines reason, affected items, risk, effectivity, and serial scope.
- 完成验证、文件/BOM/程序更新、培训和首件确认。 / Completes validation, document/BOM/program updates, training, and first-article checks.
- 管理旧库存处置、供应商同步、回滚和现场反馈。 / Manages old stock, supplier alignment, rollback, and field feedback.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：口头通知后直接切换生产。 / Changes production after verbal notice only.
- `3`：完成审批和验证，但配置或供应链切换有限。 / Completes approval and validation with limited configuration or supply transition.
- `5`：端到端影响评估、版本防错、分阶段验证、完整追溯和关闭标准。 / Provides end-to-end impact analysis, version mistake-proofing, staged validation, traceability, and closure criteria.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 在客户或法规批准前使用变更。 / Uses the change before customer or regulatory approval.
- 库存切换未记录导致混料。 / Fails to document inventory cutover and causes mixing.
