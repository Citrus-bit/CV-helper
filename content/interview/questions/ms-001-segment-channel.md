# 客群与渠道选择 / Segment and channel choice

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 一款新的职业服务预算有限，需要选择首个目标客群和获客渠道。你会如何形成并验证选择？

**English:** A new career service has a limited budget and must choose its first target segment and acquisition channel. How would you form and validate the choice?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你会如何估算可触达市场而非只看总市场？ / English: How would you estimate reachable rather than total market?
- 中文：什么结果会让你停止该渠道？ / English: What result would make you stop the channel?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 依据需求强度、支付意愿、可触达性和留存区分客群。 / Segments by need, willingness to pay, reachability, and retention.
- 比较渠道成本、反馈速度、品牌风险和规模上限。 / Compares cost, feedback speed, brand risk, and scale ceiling.
- 设计有基线、归因和停止阈值的小实验。 / Designs a small test with baseline, attribution, and stop thresholds.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：选择“所有求职者”和最热门渠道。 / Targets everyone and chooses the most popular channel.
- `3`：定义客群并试投渠道，但单位经济或验证标准有限。 / Defines a segment and channel test with limited economics or criteria.
- `5`：以需求和可触达性聚焦，量化单位经济并用阶段实验验证。 / Focuses by need and reach, quantifies unit economics, and validates in stages.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 用受保护属性进行不当排除。 / Uses protected attributes for inappropriate exclusion.
- 只优化低成本线索而忽视真实转化。 / Optimizes cheap leads instead of real conversion.
