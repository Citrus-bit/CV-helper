# 冲突需求优先级 / Prioritizing conflicting requests

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 销售要求大客户定制，运营要求降低人工量，用户研究显示核心流程仍难用。资源只能支持一项，你如何决策？

**English:** Sales wants an enterprise customization, operations wants less manual work, and research shows the core flow is hard to use. You can fund one. How do you decide?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：你最先补充哪三类证据？ / English: Which three evidence gaps would you close first?
- 中文：未被选择的诉求如何管理？ / English: How would you manage the requests not selected?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 对齐战略目标、用户影响、收入/成本、风险和可逆性。 / Aligns strategy, user impact, revenue/cost, risk, and reversibility.
- 识别强相关方声音与真实需求规模的差异。 / Distinguishes loud stakeholders from actual need size.
- 给出决定、假设、复查节点和替代缓解。 / Makes a decision with assumptions, review points, and mitigations.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：按职位高低或声音大小决定。 / Decides by seniority or volume of advocacy.
- `3`：用框架比较并作出取舍，但证据或后续管理有限。 / Uses a framework and trade-off with limited evidence or follow-through.
- `5`：补关键证据、明确战略权重、作出可解释决定并管理机会成本。 / Closes key evidence gaps, weights strategy, decides transparently, and manages opportunity cost.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 把评分框架当作自动决策器。 / Treats a scoring framework as an automatic decision maker.
- 承诺所有诉求都会很快实现。 / Promises every request will be delivered soon.
