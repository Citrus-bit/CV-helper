# 新市场进入 / New-market entry

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 公司考虑进入一个语言、招聘习惯和监管要求都不同的地区。你会如何评估吸引力并设计首个验证阶段？

**English:** A company is considering a region with different language, hiring practices, and regulations. How would you assess attractiveness and design the first validation stage?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：哪些假设最可能让总市场估算失真？ / English: Which assumptions could most distort the market estimate?
- 中文：何时应本地化，何时应改变产品本身？ / English: When is localization enough, and when must the product change?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 区分总市场、可服务市场和短期可获得市场。 / Separates total, serviceable, and near-term obtainable markets.
- 评估需求、渠道、竞争、法规、数据和运营能力。 / Assesses demand, channels, competition, regulation, data, and operating capability.
- 用受限地区/客群试点检验关键假设和退出标准。 / Tests critical assumptions with a bounded pilot and exit criteria.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只根据人口或 GDP 判断市场。 / Decides from population or GDP alone.
- `3`：覆盖市场、竞争和进入方式，但验证设计有限。 / Covers market, competition, and entry mode with limited validation.
- `5`：量化吸引力与进入成本、优先验证致命假设并设计可逆路径。 / Quantifies attraction and entry cost, tests fatal assumptions first, and keeps the path reversible.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 假设一个地区的用户行为可直接复制。 / Assumes behavior transfers unchanged across regions.
- 在合规评估前收集或传输个人数据。 / Collects or transfers personal data before compliance review.
