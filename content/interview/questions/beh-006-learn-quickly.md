# 快速学习陌生领域 / Learning an unfamiliar domain quickly

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你必须在短时间内掌握陌生领域并交付结果的经历。你如何决定学什么、向谁求证？

**English:** Tell me about a time you had to learn an unfamiliar domain quickly and deliver a result. How did you choose what to learn and whom to consult?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你如何识别自己仍然不知道的部分？ / English: How did you identify what you still did not know?
- 中文：你怎样验证所学内容可以安全应用？ / English: How did you validate that your learning was safe to apply?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 从交付目标反推最小知识范围。 / Works backward from the deliverable to the minimum knowledge needed.
- 组合一手资料、专家反馈和小规模试验。 / Combines primary sources, expert feedback, and small tests.
- 说明学习速度和结果，而非只列资料。 / Demonstrates learning speed and outcomes, not just resources consumed.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只描述搜索资料，没有应用或验证。 / Only describes searching, with no application or validation.
- `3`：形成学习计划并完成任务，但风险控制有限。 / Builds a plan and completes the task with limited risk controls.
- `5`：聚焦关键未知、快速验证、及时求助，并沉淀可复用知识。 / Targets key unknowns, validates quickly, seeks help, and captures reusable knowledge.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 夸大短期学习为专业资质。 / Overstates short learning as professional qualification.
- 在高风险领域未经审核直接应用。 / Applies unreviewed learning in a high-risk domain.
