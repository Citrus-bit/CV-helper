# 规则解释不明确 / Regulatory ambiguity

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 新业务的会计或监管处理没有直接先例，且上线时间紧。你如何形成可辩护的判断？

**English:** A new business model has no direct accounting or regulatory precedent, and launch timing is tight. How would you form a defensible judgment?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你会查阅和保留哪些权威层级的证据？ / English: Which levels of authoritative evidence would you consult and retain?
- 中文：什么不确定性必须在上线前消除？ / English: Which uncertainty must be resolved before launch?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 明确事实模式、适用范围、原则和相近先例。 / Defines facts, scope, principles, and analogous precedent.
- 及时征询法务、审计或监管专家并记录不同意见。 / Consults legal, audit, or regulatory experts and records dissent.
- 形成批准、披露、控制和触发重审的书面结论。 / Documents approval, disclosure, controls, and review triggers.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：选择最有利处理，理由是“没有明确禁止”。 / Chooses the most favorable treatment because it is not explicitly prohibited.
- `3`：研究规则并咨询专家，但事实边界或重审机制有限。 / Researches and consults with limited factual boundaries or review triggers.
- `5`：建立权威证据链、透明披露不确定性、批准控制并持续重评。 / Builds authoritative evidence, discloses uncertainty, controls approval, and reassesses.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 把非专业意见当成正式法律或会计结论。 / Treats informal input as formal legal or accounting advice.
- 在事实改变后继续沿用原判断。 / Keeps the conclusion after underlying facts change.
