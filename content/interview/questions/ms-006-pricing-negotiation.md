# 价格谈判 / Pricing negotiation

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 临近季度末，客户愿意签约但要求大幅折扣和不标准条款。你如何准备并推进谈判？

**English:** Near quarter-end, a customer will sign only with a large discount and nonstandard terms. How would you prepare and negotiate?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你的底线、可交换项和审批边界是什么？ / English: What are your walk-away point, tradable terms, and approval boundaries?
- 中文：如何评估签下坏合同的长期成本？ / English: How would you assess the long-term cost of a bad contract?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 了解客户利益、替代方案、决策人和时间压力。 / Understands interests, alternatives, decision makers, and time pressure.
- 每次让步都交换期限、范围、承诺或风险降低。 / Trades every concession for term, scope, commitment, or risk reduction.
- 遵守审批和收入确认边界，敢于退出。 / Respects approvals and revenue-recognition boundaries and can walk away.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：为冲季度数字接受任何条件。 / Accepts any terms to hit the quarter.
- `3`：准备让步区间并守住底线，但价值交换有限。 / Prepares a range and protects a floor with limited value exchange.
- `5`：以利益而非立场谈判、量化全生命周期价值，并形成合规双赢方案。 / Negotiates interests, quantifies lifetime value, and builds a compliant mutual outcome.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 越权承诺价格、服务或合同条款。 / Commits to price, service, or terms without authority.
- 隐瞒不利条款以加快内部审批。 / Conceals unfavorable terms to speed internal approval.
