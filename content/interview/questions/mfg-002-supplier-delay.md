# 关键供应商延期 / Critical supplier delay

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 单一来源的关键部件确认延期三周，可能影响客户交付。你会如何构建恢复方案并沟通承诺？

**English:** A sole-source critical component is delayed three weeks and may affect customer deliveries. How would you build a recovery plan and communicate commitments?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你如何验证供应商给出的新日期？ / English: How would you validate the supplier's revised date?
- 中文：替代料或加急运输有哪些质量与成本风险？ / English: What quality and cost risks come with substitutes or expediting?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 建立在途、库存、需求优先级和产能的事实视图。 / Builds a fact base for in-transit stock, inventory, demand priority, and capacity.
- 形成供应商恢复、替代认证、排产和客户分配多路径。 / Develops supplier recovery, alternate qualification, scheduling, and allocation paths.
- 对承诺使用有证据的区间并定期更新。 / Uses evidence-based ranges and regular updates for commitments.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只催供应商或对所有客户继续承诺原日期。 / Only pressures the supplier or keeps promising the original date.
- `3`：制定加急和排产方案，但验证与优先级规则有限。 / Expedites and replans with limited validation or prioritization rules.
- `5`：量化影响、多路径恢复、保护质量、透明分配并建立供应韧性。 / Quantifies impact, recovers through multiple paths, protects quality, allocates transparently, and improves resilience.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 未批准就替换材料或供应商。 / Substitutes material or supplier without approval.
- 通过过度承诺把风险推给客户。 / Transfers risk to customers through overpromising.
