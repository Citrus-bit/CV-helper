# 追溯与召回范围 / Traceability and recall scope

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 某原材料批次可能不合格，但系统中部分批次关系缺失。你如何确定隔离和召回范围？

**English:** A raw-material lot may be nonconforming, but some lot genealogy is missing. How would you determine quarantine and recall scope?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：在证据不足时如何应用保守边界？ / English: How would you apply conservative boundaries when evidence is incomplete?
- 中文：如何验证追溯系统整改有效？ / English: How would you validate traceability remediation?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 暂停相关放行并整合批次、时间、设备、仓位和出货记录。 / Pauses release and joins lot, time, equipment, location, and shipment records.
- 明确已知、推断和未知，按风险扩大范围。 / Separates known, inferred, and unknown and expands scope by risk.
- 做正向/反向追溯演练并修复数据控制。 / Performs forward/backward trace drills and fixes data controls.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：因系统查不到关系而假定未受影响。 / Assumes no impact because the system lacks a link.
- `3`：手工追溯并扩大隔离，但不确定性和整改验证有限。 / Traces manually and broadens quarantine with limited uncertainty or remediation testing.
- `5`：保守保护客户、证据化缩小范围、透明沟通未知并验证端到端追溯。 / Protects conservatively, narrows with evidence, communicates unknowns, and validates end-to-end traceability.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 为减少召回成本而无证据缩小范围。 / Narrows scope without evidence to reduce recall cost.
- 修改或补造历史批次记录。 / Alters or fabricates historical lot records.
