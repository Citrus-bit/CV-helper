# 定义产品成功 / Defining product success

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 一个团队准备上线“简历建议一键接受”功能。你会如何定义成功指标、护栏和观察周期？

**English:** A team plans to launch one-click acceptance of resume suggestions. How would you define success metrics, guardrails, and the observation window?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：接受率高为什么不一定代表用户价值？ / English: Why might a high acceptance rate fail to represent user value?
- 中文：你会如何检测事实错误或用户后悔？ / English: How would you detect factual errors or user regret?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 从用户任务和长期结果推导主指标。 / Derives the primary metric from the user job and durable outcome.
- 同时设置事实安全、撤销、投诉和完成时间护栏。 / Adds guardrails for factual safety, reversals, complaints, and completion time.
- 明确分群、基线、窗口和决策阈值。 / Defines cohorts, baseline, window, and decision threshold.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只看点击或接受率。 / Uses only clicks or acceptance rate.
- `3`：有主指标和护栏，但因果与观察周期有限。 / Has a primary metric and guardrails with limited causal or time thinking.
- `5`：连接用户价值、领先/滞后指标、风险护栏和明确决策规则。 / Connects user value, leading/lagging metrics, risk guardrails, and decision rules.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 用短期互动替代最终质量。 / Substitutes short-term engagement for final quality.
- 忽略不同简历类型的效果差异。 / Ignores effects across resume types.
