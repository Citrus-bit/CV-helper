# 营运资本改善 / Working-capital improvement

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 公司希望在六个月内释放营运资本，但销售担心催收影响客户，运营担心降库存影响交付。你如何制定方案？

**English:** The company wants to release working capital in six months, but sales fears collection harm and operations fears stockouts. How would you build a plan?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：你会按哪些维度拆分应收和库存？ / English: How would you segment receivables and inventory?
- 中文：如何防止期末一次性动作随后反弹？ / English: How would you prevent period-end actions from reversing later?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 按客户、账龄、争议、SKU、波动和服务水平细分。 / Segments customers, aging, disputes, SKUs, variability, and service levels.
- 联合解决发票质量、条款、预测和慢动库存根因。 / Addresses invoicing, terms, forecasting, and slow stock jointly.
- 用现金、服务、毛利和持续性护栏管理。 / Manages cash with service, margin, and sustainability guardrails.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：全面延付供应商、强催客户或统一砍库存。 / Delays all suppliers, pressures all customers, or cuts inventory uniformly.
- `3`：识别主要杠杆并跨团队推进，但分群或持续性有限。 / Identifies levers and collaborates with limited segmentation or durability.
- `5`：基于分群解决根因、平衡关系与服务，并建立不反弹机制。 / Solves root causes by segment, balances relationships and service, and prevents rebound.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 以损害关键供应商为代价美化期末现金。 / Improves period-end cash by harming critical suppliers.
- 忽略有争议应收与真实可收回性。 / Ignores disputed receivables and collectability.
