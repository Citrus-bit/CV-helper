# 改善低效流程 / Improving an inefficient process

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你发现重复、等待或返工并改进流程的经历。你如何证明改善没有转移问题？

**English:** Tell me about a time you reduced repetition, waiting, or rework. How did you prove the improvement did not simply move the problem elsewhere?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：改进前的基线是什么？ / English: What was the baseline before the change?
- 中文：谁受到流程变化影响，你如何让他们参与？ / English: Who was affected, and how did you involve them?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 用端到端指标而非局部速度定义问题。 / Defines the problem with end-to-end rather than local metrics.
- 先小范围验证，再推广和标准化。 / Pilots before scaling and standardizing.
- 同时跟踪效率、质量和人员负担。 / Tracks efficiency, quality, and workload together.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只声称“优化很多”，没有基线或结果。 / Claims major improvement with no baseline or result.
- `3`：有清楚改变和结果，但副作用验证有限。 / Shows a clear change and result with limited side-effect checks.
- `5`：建立基线、共同设计、试点验证，并持续监控端到端效果。 / Baselines, co-designs, pilots, and monitors end-to-end effects.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 把工作转嫁给下游却称为提效。 / Shifts work downstream while claiming efficiency.
- 自动化了错误或未授权流程。 / Automates a flawed or unauthorized process.
