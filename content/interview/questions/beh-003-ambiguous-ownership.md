# 模糊职责中的推进 / Acting under unclear ownership

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 当一个重要问题没有明确负责人时，请讲一次你如何界定边界并推动解决的经历。

**English:** Tell me about a time an important problem had no clear owner. How did you define boundaries and move it toward resolution?

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：哪些事项由你承担，哪些必须升级确认？ / English: What did you own, and what required escalation?
- 中文：你如何避免临时接手变成永久职责混乱？ / English: How did you prevent temporary ownership from becoming permanent confusion?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 先确认影响、决策权和现有责任关系。 / Clarifies impact, decision rights, and existing responsibilities.
- 在授权边界内采取可逆行动并及时同步。 / Takes reversible action within authority and communicates promptly.
- 解决当下问题后补充长期责任机制。 / Establishes durable ownership after resolving the immediate issue.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：等待别人处理，或越权决定且不沟通。 / Waits passively or makes unauthorized decisions without communication.
- `3`：主动协调并解决问题，但长期责任仍不清楚。 / Coordinates a solution, but durable ownership remains unclear.
- `5`：平衡行动与授权，解决问题并建立清晰的责任和升级路径。 / Balances action with authority and creates clear ownership and escalation paths.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 把“主人翁精神”描述成绕过审批。 / Equates ownership with bypassing approval.
- 抢占他人成果或忽略专业边界。 / Claims others' work or ignores specialist boundaries.
