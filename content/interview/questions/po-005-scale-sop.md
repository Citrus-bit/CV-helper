# 将临时流程规模化 / Scaling an ad hoc process

## 问题 / Question
**中文：** 请讲一次你把依赖少数熟练人员的临时流程变成可规模化运营机制的经历。

**English:** Tell me about turning an ad hoc process dependent on a few experts into a scalable operating mechanism.

## 追问 / Follow-ups
- 中文：哪些步骤需要标准化，哪些需要保留判断空间？ / English: What needed standardization, and where was judgment still necessary?
- 中文：你如何验证新人可以正确执行？ / English: How did you verify a new operator could execute correctly?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals
- 记录需求量、周期、错误和例外作为基线。 / Baselines demand, cycle time, errors, and exceptions.
- 设计 SOP、培训、权限、质检和升级路径。 / Designs SOPs, training, permissions, QA, and escalation.
- 以质量和容量共同衡量规模化效果。 / Measures scale with both quality and capacity.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors
- `1`：只增加人手或写文档。 / Only adds headcount or writes documentation.
- `3`：建立流程和培训，能改善效率但例外管理有限。 / Builds process and training with efficiency gains but limited exception handling.
- `5`：端到端标准化、验证能力、管理例外，并用运营指标持续迭代。 / Standardizes end to end, validates capability, manages exceptions, and iterates with metrics.

## 风险项 / Risks
- 过度标准化导致高风险例外被忽略。 / Over-standardizes and hides high-risk exceptions.
- 指标只追求处理量而牺牲质量。 / Optimizes throughput at the expense of quality.
