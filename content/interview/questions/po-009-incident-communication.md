# 服务异常沟通 / Service incident communication

## 问题 / Question

**中文：** 服务发生影响用户但原因未明的异常。作为运营或产品负责人，你如何在不猜测的情况下对内外沟通？

**English:** A service issue affects users, but the cause is unknown. As an operations or product lead, how would you communicate internally and externally without speculating?

## 追问 / Follow-ups

- 中文：第一次公告应包含和避免什么？ / English: What should the first notice include and avoid?
- 中文：更新频率如何确定？ / English: How would you set the update cadence?

## 优秀信号 / Strong signals

- 区分已知、未知、当前影响和正在采取的行动。 / Separates known facts, unknowns, impact, and actions underway.
- 按受众需要统一事实源、负责人和更新时间。 / Uses a shared source of truth, owner, and update time by audience.
- 恢复后说明验证、补偿和后续改进。 / Communicates validation, remediation, and follow-up after recovery.

## 评分锚点 / Scoring anchors

- `1`：沉默等待根因，或提前作无证据承诺。 / Waits silently for root cause or makes unsupported promises.
- `3`：及时说明影响并持续更新，但受众或验证细节有限。 / Communicates impact and updates with limited audience or validation detail.
- `5`：透明管理不确定性、统一信息、保护用户并闭环后续。 / Manages uncertainty transparently, aligns information, protects users, and closes the loop.

## 风险项 / Risks

- 未确认就归因于供应商或某个人。 / Blames a vendor or person before confirmation.
- 公告泄露安全细节或个人数据。 / Discloses security details or personal data.
